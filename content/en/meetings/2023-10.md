Session of talks on "Introduction to Object-Oriented Programming with Python" and "Python Introspection and Sandbox Evasion".

In the first talk, Martín gave us a simple presentation on OOP (Object-Oriented Programming) in Python aimed at people with a basic level of programming.

In the second talk, Eloy showed us, in a talk titled "Python sandboxing and black magic", how a sandbox should NOT be built and how easy it is to attack one for someone with ingenuity and knowledge. Python is a very dynamic and accessible language, too much so...

If you couldn't attend the talks, don't worry. You can watch the videos on Python Coruña's [Youtube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@PythonCoruna):

- [Introduction to object-oriented programming with Python](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=J6n-Bm8LYoM)
- [Python Introspection and Sandbox Evasion](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bnujnTG9XfM)
