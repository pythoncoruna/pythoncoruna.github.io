We’re back from our summer holidays and we’re kicking things off in style with a talk sponsored by the team at [YepCode](https://yepcode.io/).

They’ll give us a brief introduction to their tool and demonstrate, through a couple of practical use cases (one of which uses the OpenAI API that’s been all the rage lately), just how useful it can be for both developers taking their first steps and those with more experience.

We’ll also be treated to some tapas and drinks during the talk, so it’s very important that you sign up for this event so that we can keep track of who’s actually attending and distinguish them from the bar’s other customers.

We’ll also be giving away a couple of annual licences kindly provided by the team at [JetBrains](https://www.jetbrains.com/), so you can use any of their products (preferably PyCharm, as this group is all about Python).

Last but not least, we’ll be giving away two tickets to [Atlántica Conf 2023](https://atlanticaconf.com/), which will take place on 22 and 23 September at the Faculty of Computer Science in A Coruña.

For any further details about the event, please feel free to contact us using any of the methods listed on our [Linktree](https://linktr.ee/pythoncoruna).

Thank you all for being part of this unforgettable event! With this talk, we start the 23/24
season of Python Coruña!
