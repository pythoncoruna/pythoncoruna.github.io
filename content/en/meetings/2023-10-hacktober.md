## What is Hacktoberfest?

**Hacktoberfest** is an annual event organized by *DigitalOcean* to promote open source.
With HacktoberfestES we want to adapt the global event to the Spanish-speaking community and make participation easier by selecting some projects and creating spaces and activities where people can meet each other and learn.

## Who can participate?

All levels and programming languages are welcome. It doesn't matter if you're a beginner: this event exists precisely to help newcomers overcome the barrier of their first contributions.

You can find more information about HacktoberfestES at this link: [https://hacktoberfestes.dev/](https://hacktoberfestes.dev/)
