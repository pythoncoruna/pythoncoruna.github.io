Talks session on "Type Hints with Python" and "Generators and Iterators".

In the first talk, Pedro explains how *Type Hints* work in Python and how they help us better specify the typing of our programs.


In the second talk, Alejandro explains what *iterators* and *generators* are, how they are implemented in Python, and some details about how they work.

If you couldn't attend the talks, don't worry. You can watch the videos on the [Python Coruña YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@PythonCoruna):

- [Type Hints con Python](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=paSX8Zc8Bb0)
- [Generadores e iteradores](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8ENNvOiEwKU)
